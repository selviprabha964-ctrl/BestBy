# BestBy

Helps people track food expiry dates and reduce household food waste — get a nudge before things go off, and a recipe to use them up instead.

##  Problem

- Households routinely lose track of what's in the fridge and pantry, and forget what's approaching its expiry date until it's already spoiled.
- The average household throws away a significant share of the food it buys, wasting both money and groceries that were never used.
- There's no simple way to see, at a glance, what needs to be used soon — expiry dates are buried on packaging, easy to miss, and easy to forget once food is moved to a fridge shelf or freezer.
- Even when people know something is expiring soon, they often don't have a quick idea of what to actually cook with it, so it gets wasted anyway.

##  Solution

BestBy turns expiry tracking into a habit instead of a guessing game:

- **Log items in seconds** — scan a barcode or add manually, with expiry dates auto-estimated by category.
- **See urgency at a glance** — a simple color-coded system (fresh / use soon / use today) replaces squinting at printed dates.
- **Get nudged before it's too late** — items are surfaced by how soon they need to be used, not buried in a flat list.
- **Turn "about to expire" into dinner** — recipe suggestions are generated directly from what's expiring soonest, closing the loop between tracking and actually using the food.
- **Make the impact visible** — a running total of food and money saved reinforces the habit over time.

6. Empty state — first-run onboarding

##  Core Features

- Track expiry dates by scanning a barcode or entering manually
- Color-coded urgency (fresh / use soon / use today)
- Smart expiry estimates by category
- Recipe suggestions prioritizing soon-to-expire items
- Monthly waste and savings tracking
